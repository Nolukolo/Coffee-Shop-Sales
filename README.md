# Bright Coffee Shop — Sales Analysis (BRIGHTLIGHT)

**Goal:** uncover actionable insights from historical point-of-sale data to support a new CEO in growing revenue and improving product performance.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional sales data from **Bright Coffee Shop**.
The aim is to identify **top revenue drivers, peak sales times, and underperforming products** — and to present clear recommendations for growth.

The analysis combined **SQL for data processing**, **Excel/Power BI for visualization**, and **Miro/PowerPoint for planning & presentation**.

---

## Key Insights

* **Morning dominance:** Over **55% of revenue (R388K)** comes from the morning period, led by Coffee (R144K) and Tea (R104K).
* **Afternoon strength:** Sales reached **R163K**, still less than half of morning performance.
* **Evening weakness:** Revenue falls to **R144K**, mainly Tea and Chocolate.
* **Day-of-week trends:** Fridays and Mondays show the highest sales, while **Tuesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays consistently underperform**.
* **Growth momentum:** Revenue **doubled from Jan–Jun (R81K → R166K)**, with Coffee alone adding R33K.
* **Top products:** Items like *Sustainably Grown Organic Lg* and *Dark Chocolate Lg* generated over R21K each and sold 4,000+ units.
* **Bottom products:** Premium niche items earned less than R1,400, with fewer than 155 units sold.

---

## Recommendations

* **Product Strategy**

  * Focus on **Coffee, Tea, Chocolate, and Bakery** as core categories.
  * Introduce **combos/bundles** (e.g., Coffee + Scone).
  * Reposition or phase out consistently low-demand products.

* **Time-Based Promotions**

  * **Morning Rush Specials** to maximize peak revenue.
  * **Afternoon Combos** to encourage repeat purchases.
  * Scale back or adapt **night operations**.

* **Day-Based Campaigns**

  * Target slow days (Tues, Sat, Sun) with discounts or loyalty offers.
  * Double down on **Fridays and Mondays**, the highest-volume days.
